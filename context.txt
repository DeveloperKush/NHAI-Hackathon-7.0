### SLIDE 2: The Core Problem
* **Context**: NHAI (National Highways Authority of India) construction sites and toll plazas are often in remote areas with poor or non-existent internet connectivity.
* **Key Pain Points**:
  1. **Proxy Attendance / Identity Fraud**: Traditional card-based or simple 2D photo attendance systems are easily spoofed using a picture or video on a phone.
  2. **Connectivity Failures**: Cloud-based face recognition systems completely fail when there is no network, leading to manual entries or delayed work.
  3. **High Infrastructure Costs**: Specialized biometric scanners (fingerprint/iris) are expensive to deploy, maintain, and scale across thousands of remote highway sites.
  4. **Harsh Lighting Environments**: High sun glare during the day, low light during nights, and dusty outdoor conditions at construction sites degrade standard facial recognition accuracy.

---

### SLIDE 3: The Innovation & Solution
* **Core Value Proposition**: A cross-platform (Android & iOS) mobile application running **100% on-device** face authentication, including 3D liveness detection, with zero dependence on cloud compute or internet connectivity.
* **Key Pillars**:
  - **Edge AI Inference**: Face embeddings extracted locally in under 200ms.
  - **4-Factor Liveness**: Real-time spoof detection running locally.
  - **Offline-First Registry & DB**: Local enrollment, verification, and encrypted storage.
  - **Resilient Sync Architecture**: Logs automatically queued offline and synced to AWS when online.

### SLIDE 5: 3D Liveness Detection Pipeline (Spoof Prevention)
* **Mechanism**: Runs MediaPipe Face Mesh WebAssembly inside a hidden, hardware-accelerated WKWebView/WebView.
* **Spoof Checks**:
  1. **Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) Detection**: Recognizes active blinks to prevent static photo spoofs.
  2. **Head Yaw / Turn Check**: Measures head angles to ensure a real 3D head is moving in front of the camera.
  3. **Passive 3D Depth Check**: Evaluates the z-variance of facial landmarks to distinguish between a flat 2D surface (photo/phone screen) and a natural 3D face structure.
* **Optimization**: Uses an inline base64-injected graph and an XHR/fetch polyfill for WKWebView, allowing files to load securely from local storage.

---

### SLIDE 6: Edge AI Face Recognition
* **Model**: **GhostFaceNet INT8 Quantized model** (~1 MB).
* **Execution**: Runs synchronously via `react-native-fast-tflite` utilizing the high-performance C++ JSI (JavaScript Interface) bridge directly on the device GPU/CPU.
* **Key Tech Specs**:
  - **Input Tensor**: 112x112 pixel resolution (normalized to [-1.0, 1.0]).
  - **Output Embedding**: 512-dimensional unit L2-normalized vector.
  - **Performance**: Sub-200ms local inference time.
  - **Quantization**: High-fidelity INT8 scale & zero-point quantization/dequantization done natively with zero cloud roundtrips.

---

### SLIDE 7: Robust Preprocessing for Indian Outdoor Environments
* **Challenge**: Wildly varying light conditions on national highways (harsh sunlight, shadows, nighttime).
* **The Preprocessing Pipeline**:
  - **JPEG Decode**: decodes raw bytes on-the-fly via `jpeg-js`.
  - **CLAHE (Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization)**: Advanced contrast adjustment applied in 8x8 grids to normalize lighting across faces, eliminating shadows and low-light degradation.
  - **Bilinear Crop & Resize**: Automatically crops and normalizes the face to 112x112 grayscale pixels for the recognition engine.

---

### SLIDE 8: Offline Storage & Enterprise Security
* **Registry & Databases**:
  - **Enrolled Database**: Uses `expo-sqlite` to store enrolled facial embeddings locally.
  - **Security / Encryption**: Embeddings and logs are encrypted using **AES-256-GCM/CBC** via `react-native-encrypted-storage` linked to the iOS Keychain and Android Keystore.
  - **Multi-Frame Enrollment**: Enrollment captures 3-5 distinct frames, averages the embedding vectors, and registers a stable face profile.

---

### SLIDE 9: Resilient Sync Queue & AWS Integration
* **Data Logging**: Every auth attempt (success/failure) generates a secure log containing the timestamp, similarity score, device ID, GPS coordinates, and a compressed image thumbnail.
* **Sync Rules (Zero-Loss Guarantee)**:
  - Logs are queued in SQLite.
  - An auto-sync listener detects network reconnection (`@react-native-community/netinfo`) and automatically pushes the queue to a POST `/api/sync` AWS endpoint.
  - **Crucial Purge Rule**: Client deletes local logs *only* after receiving a verified HTTP 200 response with matching `received_logs` IDs from the server. Keeps queue intact during server errors (5xx) or dropped packets.

---

### SLIDE 10: Multiplatform Architecture & EAS Cloud
* **CNG Workflow**: Built on Expo SDK 50 managed workflow utilizing Continuous Native Generation.
* **iOS Integration Configured**:
